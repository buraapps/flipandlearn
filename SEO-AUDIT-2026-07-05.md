# Flip & Learn — SEO & Visibility Audit

**Date:** 2026-07-05 · **Phase:** 1 (Audit, read-only) · **Repo:** /Volumes/Data/BuraApps/web/ · **Live:** https://flipandlearn.app

---

## 1. Executive summary

1. **You cannot currently see website→store conversions at all.** Store links carry no Apple campaign token (`ct`/`pt`), no Play install referrer, and the Google Ads tag never fires a click event on any store badge. Even if blog traffic *were* converting, the data could not correlate. Fix attribution first (SEO-002) — everything else is guesswork until then.
2. **Your only download-intent page is an orphan.** `/en/learn-english/` and all 4 blog posts receive zero internal links from any of the 9 homepages; they are reachable only via sitemap.xml. Add nav/footer links (SEO-001) and clone the learn-english template into the other locales (§3 roadmap).
3. **The homepage targets no queries.** Title = "Flip & Learn — 9 Languages · Ages 4+", H1 = brand only, description has no "kids / learning app / language" keywords in any locale (SEO-007).

**Hypothesis verdict — partially agree.** The blog *is* research/commercial-investigation intent ("best free matching games", "kostenlose Kinderspiele"), but the articles already carry strong, contextual store CTAs (2 CTA rows each), so "blog lacks a conversion path" is not the explanation. The stronger explanations visible in the repo: (a) attribution is impossible today, so downloads may correlate invisibly; (b) the single transactional page (/en/learn-english/) has no internal link equity and covers 1 of 9 locales; (c) Cloudflare Web Analytics counts bot/crawler hits that never become store visits. Conclusion: keep the blog, fix measurement + internal linking, and shift new-content effort to transactional landing pages.

## 2. Findings by priority

### P1 — Blocking visibility or conversion

#### SEO-001 · Money pages orphaned — no internal links from any homepage
- **Files:** `index.html` nav (lines 385–392) and footer (lines 692–698); propagates to all 9 generated locale homepages.
- **Description:** `grep -c 'blog\|learn-english'` over `index.html`, `de/index.html`, `ro/index.html`, `hu/index.html`, `es/index.html` returns **0** everywhere. `/en/learn-english/` and the 4 blog posts are linked only from `sitemap.xml` (plus one blog↔landing pair: EN blog line 200 → learn-english; learn-english line 456 → EN blog).
- **Why it matters:** Orphan pages get minimal crawl priority and no PageRank from the homepage (the page with all the backlinks). Homepage visitors also never discover the content that answers their pre-download questions.
- **Fix (Phase 2):** Add T-dictionary-driven links in root `index.html` — a "Blog" footer link per locale where an article exists (en/ro/de/hu) and a "Learn English" link (or the locale-appropriate landing page once built). Because per-locale pages are generated, the link targets must be locale-conditional: add per-locale T keys (e.g. `nav.blog`, `link.blogUrl`) and have `build-locales.js` skip/rewrite for locales without a blog. Never hand-edit `de/index.html` etc.

#### SEO-002 · Zero website→store attribution (explains the "traffic ≠ downloads" mystery)
- **Files:** `index.html:486–487` (hero badges), `en/learn-english/index.html:280–285, 443–448`, all 4 blog posts lines ~218–221 and ~300–305.
- **Description:** Three independent gaps:
  1. No `gtag('event', ...)` anywhere in the repo (grep: 0 hits) — the consent-gated Ads tag `AW-18076192122` loads but store-badge clicks are never recorded as events/conversions.
  2. App Store links are bare `https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761679251` — no Apple campaign token (`?pt=<providerToken>&ct=<campaign>&mt=8`), so App Store Connect Analytics cannot attribute installs to the site.
  3. Play links are bare — no `&referrer=utm_source%3Dflipandlearn.app%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_campaign%3D<page>` install-referrer param, so Play Console shows these installs as untagged.
- **Why it matters:** This is the most likely explanation for "Cloudflare shows traffic but store downloads don't correlate": the correlation is *unmeasurable* with the current links. Cloudflare page views also include bots.
- **Fix (Phase 2):** (a) Add a small click handler that fires `gtag('event','store_click',{store, page})` when consent granted (degrade silently otherwise); (b) append `ct=<page-slug>&pt=…&mt=8` to every App Store link, per page; (c) append URL-encoded `referrer` UTM params to every Play link, per page. Apply in root `index.html` source + the 5 standalone pages.

#### SEO-003 · Factual error: "Polish" instead of "Portuguese" (visible FAQ + FAQPage JSON-LD)
- **File:** `en/learn-english/index.html:108` (JSON-LD) and `:429` (visible answer).
- **Description:** "supports 9 languages including Spanish, French, German, Italian, **Polish**, Hungarian, Romanian, and Arabic." The app has Portuguese, not Polish (see root `index.html:69` `inLanguage` list).
- **Why it matters:** A parent searching "app to teach my kid Polish" downloads, finds no Polish, leaves a 1-star review; structured data with wrong facts also erodes trust with Google's quality systems.
- **Fix:** Replace "Polish" with "Portuguese" in both places.

#### SEO-004 · Root schema declares the app costs €2.99 (it's free-to-download)
- **File:** `index.html:74–81` (MobileApplication `offers.price: "2.99"`), replicated to all 9 locale homepages.
- **Description:** The root JSON-LD sells the app at €2.99, while `/en/learn-english/` correctly declares `price: "0"` with an IAP description (`en/learn-english/index.html:49–54`). Two pages on the same site describe the same app with contradictory prices.
- **Why it matters:** If Google renders a rich result with "€2.99", price-sensitive parents skip the click — the exact opposite of the download-conversion goal. Contradictory structured data across pages can also suppress rich results entirely.
- **Fix:** In root `index.html`, change offers to `price: "0"` + a description mirroring the learn-english wording (English + 3 categories free; one-time €2.99 unlock behind a parental gate). Keep `priceValidUntil` only if a price is stated.

#### SEO-005 · Compliance (c): age framing inconsistent across the site
- **Files:** `index.html:6` (title "Ages 4+"), `:84` (`suggestedMinAge: 4`), `:173/665` (FAQ: "start at 4 … through age 8 or so"), T-dict `b1` in all locales ("Ab 4 Jahren" etc.), level cards `lv1d–lv5d` ("Ages 12+" for Master); `en/learn-english/index.html:6,76,277,409` ("ages 3–7"); `en|ro|de` blogs ("ages 4–10", e.g. `en/blog/.../index.html:184,200,289`); `hu` blog ("4–10 éves", `hu/blog/.../index.html:7` — **correct per the Hungarian rule**).
- **Description:** Four different framings coexist: 4+ (home), 3–7 (learn-english), 4–8 (home FAQ), 4–10 (EN/RO/DE blogs). Per BuraApps copy rules the general framing should be ~3–7 (4–10 only for Hungarian). **[REVISED 2026-09-06: the target is now 4–12 for ALL locales — converged site-wide (T dict b1/faq.a6 ×21, shared FAQPage JSON-LD, all 6 blogs, learn-english, promo, og-preview, privacy p.intro2). The ~3–7 rule and the Hungarian 4–10 exception are obsolete; do NOT re-converge to 3–7. Level-card descriptors (lv1d–lv5d) and suggestedMinAge:4 stay as-is. Store content ratings (Apple 4+, Google PEGI 3) are a separate concept — never touch those for marketing framing.]**
- **Why it matters:** Compliance rule violation; also confuses Google's entity understanding (three different age ranges in three FAQPage/schema blocks) and confuses parents.
- **Fix (Phase 2, needs your sign-off on target wording):** converge general pages on ~3–7 (title badge `b1`, `suggestedMinAge: 3`, FAQ `faq.a6`, EN/RO/DE blog copy), keep 4–10 in the HU article. The level-card descriptors (9+/12+) are feature facts, not marketing age framing — probably acceptable, but flag to you: "Ages 12+ — Word only" sits oddly on a site claiming 3–7.

#### SEO-006 · Compliance (d): Apple-emoji glyphs rasterized into image assets — verify
- **Files:** `og-image.jpg` (65 KB, referenced by every homepage + blog og:image), `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon.png`, `flip_and_learn_feature_graphic.png`.
- **Description:** Visual inspection of `og-image.jpg` shows the app-icon artwork contains Apple-style emoji renders (red apple, rocket, glowing star, open book) rasterized into the bitmap. The live HTML pages use emoji as *text* (fine); the violation risk is in the baked image assets.
- **Why it matters:** BuraApps rule (d) prohibits Apple emoji rasterized into image assets (Apple's emoji artwork is not licensed for standalone image use).
- **Fix:** Confirm the provenance of the icon artwork. If those glyphs are Apple emoji, re-render the og-image/icon with the app's own card illustrations or openly licensed emoji (Noto/Twemoji). If they're custom artwork, dismiss this finding and note it in the compliance log.

### P2 — High-leverage improvements

#### SEO-007 · Homepage title/H1/description carry no search intent, in any locale
- **Files:** `index.html:6–7` (en), derived per-locale in `build-locales.js:97–109` (`title = "Flip & Learn — {b2} · {b1}"`, `description = hero.sub`).
- **Description:** Every locale title is brand + "9 Languages · Ages 4+". H1 (`index.html:475`) is just "Flip & Learn". Descriptions (~80 chars) never contain "app", "kids/children", "learn", or a language name in query form.
- **Why it matters:** The homepage — the page with all the domain's authority — can rank only for brand queries. "Word card game for kids", "language learning game for kids" etc. are uncontested by any page.
- **Fix:** Add dedicated per-locale T keys `meta.title` / `meta.desc` (e.g. EN: "Flip & Learn — Word Card Game for Kids · Learn 9 Languages Through Play, Ages 3–7"; ~55–60 chars ideal) and use them in `derivedMeta()` instead of composing from badges. Localize the query phrasing per market (you verify hu/ro yourself). Consider making H1 "Flip & Learn — word card game for kids" via a new `hero.h1` key.

#### SEO-008 · Only one transactional landing page, in one language — build the rest
- **Files:** template = `en/learn-english/index.html`; hero-composite generator = `scripts/build-hero-composite.py` (explicitly documented as reproducible for other languages, lines 20–22).
- **Description:** The site's page-to-intent map (full table in §3): 9 homepages = brand/navigational; 4 blog posts = informational/commercial-investigation; **1** page (`/en/learn-english/`) = transactional download intent. Queries like "Englisch lernen App Kinder", "app para aprender inglés niños", "învață engleza copii", "angol tanulás gyerekeknek app" have zero coverage despite the audience already having a localized homepage + localized store badges.
- **Why it matters:** These are the "ready to install" query clusters — exactly the visibility→download conversion the site currently lacks.
- **Fix:** Clone the learn-english template per §3 roadmap. It is a standalone template (not `build-locales.js`-generated); each clone is a static page + localized hero composite. Keep the corrected schema pattern (price 0, FAQPage) from this template.

#### SEO-009 · No blog index; `/en/blog/` etc. are 404s
- **Files:** no `*/blog/index.html` exists (only per-article directories).
- **Description:** Articles live at `/en/blog/<slug>/` but `/en/blog/` returns GitHub-Pages 404. No "Blog" navigation entry anywhere (ties into SEO-001).
- **Why it matters:** No hub page to accumulate internal links and no crawl path as the article count grows; users who trim the URL hit a 404.
- **Fix:** Add minimal `/{en,ro,de,hu}/blog/index.html` hub pages (title, list of articles with dates/descriptions) and link them from the footer via T keys.

#### SEO-010 · RO/DE/HU blog translations have no hreflang cluster
- **Files:** `ro/blog/aplicatii-gratuite-copii/index.html`, `de/blog/kostenlose-kinderspiele/index.html`, `hu/blog/ingyenes-gyerekjatekok/index.html` — 0 `hreflang` links each (grep confirmed).
- **Description:** The three articles are structural translations of each other (identical H2 skeleton, same line numbers). The EN article is a *different* piece ("matching games" listicle) — related but not an equivalent.
- **Why it matters:** Without hreflang, Google may serve the DE article to Austrian users searching in Hungarian border regions, or treat the three as competing near-duplicates. With it, each ranks in its own market.
- **Fix:** Add reciprocal `hreflang="ro|de|hu"` + `x-default` (pick RO or the future EN equivalent) link tags across the three. Do **not** include the EN matching-games article in this cluster — it's a different topic.

#### SEO-011 · Schema gaps: BreadcrumbList missing everywhere; author is a faceless Organization
- **Files:** all 4 blog posts (BlogPosting `author` = Organization "BuraApps", e.g. `en/blog/.../index.html:45–49`); `en/learn-english/index.html` (no BreadcrumbList); no BreadcrumbList anywhere on the site.
- **Description:** Blog posts already have BlogPosting + FAQPage (good). Missing: BreadcrumbList (Home → Blog → Article; Home → Learn English), and a Person author despite the site's whole E-E-A-T story being "made by a parent, system test engineer in Regensburg" (`index.html:673`).
- **Why it matters:** Breadcrumbs improve SERP display and internal-structure understanding; a named Person author with a matching visible byline is a stronger experience signal than an org name, especially for "best apps for kids" content where Google scrutinizes trust.
- **Fix:** Add BreadcrumbList JSON-LD to the 5 standalone pages; change `author` to `{"@type":"Person","name":"<name>", "jobTitle":"…", "url":"https://flipandlearn.app/#about"}` and add a visible byline near the publish date (currently `post-meta`, line 171, has date only).

#### SEO-012 · AggregateRating: only add if store-sourced (policy risk otherwise)
- **Files:** root `index.html` MobileApplication block (58–92); `en/learn-english/index.html` SoftwareApplication (33–56).
- **Description:** No AggregateRating anywhere today. It is a legitimate rich-result boost (stars in SERP) **only** if the values are real, current App Store / Play ratings and the page shows them visibly.
- **Why it matters / risk:** Self-declared or stale numbers violate Google's structured-data policy (self-serving reviews) and can earn a manual action. Ratings hardcoded once will drift from the stores.
- **Fix:** If (and only if) the app has a meaningful rating count in at least one store: add `aggregateRating` with the store's exact value + count, display it visibly next to the badges ("★ 4.8 on the App Store"), and add a release-checklist item to re-sync it. Otherwise skip.

#### SEO-013 · Currency inconsistency: "$2.99" in the EN blog vs "€2.99" sitewide
- **File:** `en/blog/best-free-matching-games-kids/index.html:205`.
- **Description:** The EN article says "one-time $2.99 purchase"; every other page (including the EN homepage) says €2.99.
- **Fix:** Pick one framing for the EN article — either "€2.99" or currency-neutral "a small one-time purchase". (Actual store price is region-dependent anyway; neutral wording ages best.)

#### SEO-014 · Platform-aware CTA only half-implemented, and only on homepages
- **Files:** `index.html:183` (platform sniff) + `:245–247` (CSS badge reorder); absent from `en/learn-english/index.html` and all blog posts.
- **Description:** The homepage detects iOS/Android and reorders/scales the matching badge (good, subtle). The transactional landing page and the blogs — the pages organic visitors actually land on — always show both badges in fixed order. `apple-itunes-app` smart-banner meta is present on all pages (good), but that helps iOS Safari only.
- **Why it matters:** On mobile (most parent traffic), one of the two badges is always wrong. One fewer decision = more store clicks.
- **Fix:** Copy the 3-line platform-sniff script + order/scale CSS into the learn-english template and blog template. Optionally add a single primary "Download free" button above the badge pair that links to the platform-matched store (with the SEO-002 tracking params).

#### SEO-015 · /en/learn-english/ ships ~2.8 MB of PNG screenshots; hero is a 483 KB `fetchpriority=high` PNG
- **Files:** `en/learn-english/img/`: hero 483 KB (line 289), levels 810 KB, profiles 648 KB, celebration 477 KB, gameplay 394 KB (lines 359–372).
- **Description:** All five images are full-size PNGs; the gallery ones render at ~220–320 px wide. The homepage solved this exact problem with jpg+webp `<picture>` (e.g. `index.html:501–507`); the landing page didn't. Width/height attributes are present (no CLS) and gallery images are lazy (good) — the issue is pure bytes/LCP.
- **Why it matters:** This is the page you want ranking and converting on mobile; a ~500 KB LCP image on 4G measurably hurts both Core Web Vitals ranking input and bounce.
- **Fix:** Generate WebP (and downscaled 2×-display-size) variants — same `og-image class of problem` as before; wrap in `<picture>` like the homepage. Expected: ~2.8 MB → ~300–400 KB.

### P3 — Polish

#### SEO-016 · 950 KB `icon.png` referenced as favicon on every page; 681 KB unused feature graphic
- **Files:** `index.html:11–12` (`<link rel="icon" href="/icon.png">` — 949,817 bytes, no `sizes`), replicated to all generated pages; `flip_and_learn_feature_graphic.png` (681 KB) referenced by no HTML.
- **Why it matters:** Some browsers/crawlers fetch the largest declared icon; ~1 MB for a favicon is waste on every first visit. The feature graphic is repo dead weight (deploy payload if Pages serves the whole repo).
- **Fix:** Export a ≤48 KB 192×192 (or 512×512) icon for that link; optionally add `sizes="192x192"`. Move the feature graphic out of the web root (or accept it as store-asset storage, but note it).

#### SEO-017 · Sitemap `lastmod` is always the build date
- **File:** `build-locales.js:65–67` (`isoToday()`), used for every URL (`:557` etc.).
- **Why it matters:** Every rebuild stamps all 41 URLs "modified today", teaching Google to ignore your lastmod entirely — which hurts when you *do* publish something new.
- **Fix:** Keep a small `lastmod` map in `build-locales.js` (per template/article, bumped when content actually changes), or derive from git log per source file.

#### SEO-018 · All blog posts share the generic homepage og:image
- **Files:** blog posts line 23 (`og:image` = `/og-image.jpg`).
- **Fix:** One branded per-article image (even a title-card variant) improves social/Discover CTR. Also add `og:image:width/height` on articles (root has them, articles don't).

#### SEO-019 · Articles show a publish date but no author byline
- **Files:** blog posts line 171 (`post-meta`: date + reading time only).
- **Fix:** Add "By <name>, parent & developer of Flip & Learn" linked to the homepage About section; pairs with SEO-011's Person schema. Also decide a dateModified policy when articles get touched.

#### SEO-020 · Oversized carousel screenshots on the homepage
- **Files:** `index.html:502` (`screen2_game` 1198×2603 displayed at 195 px, 85 KB webp), `:505` (`screen4_professions` similar).
- **Fix:** Add ~400 px-wide variants via `srcset`. Minor — webp already keeps these tolerable, and they're lazy.

#### SEO-021 · No web app manifest
- **Description:** `theme-color`, apple-touch-icon and favicons exist; no `site.webmanifest`/`manifest.json` in the repo.
- **Fix:** Optional: add a minimal manifest (name, icons, theme). Low SEO impact; slight PWA/Android polish. (An Android "install app" banner for the *native* app isn't achievable via manifest — badges + SEO-014 are the Android path.)

#### SEO-022 · Homepage HTML is ~134 KB because all 9 locales' T dictionary ships on every page
- **Files:** `index.html:729–1045` (T dict), duplicated into each of the 9 generated homepages.
- **Description:** Every locale page carries all 9 translations so the client-side switcher can re-render without navigation. Gzip brings this to ~30 KB — acceptable, and the switcher UX depends on it.
- **Fix:** No action recommended now; note it as the known cost of the current i18n design. (If it ever grows, the switcher could navigate to the target locale URL instead of re-rendering, letting each page ship one locale.)

**Explicitly checked, no finding:** "memory" absent from every title/H1/URL (body-text uses are within policy) · no "completely free" claims in any of the 9 languages — free framing everywhere is correctly "English (+3 categories) free, one-time unlock for the rest" · hreflang on the 9 homepages is complete, bidirectional, with x-default (generated block verified in `de/index.html:20–30`) · legal pages have correct per-locale canonicals + hreflang · `/en/index.html` is a proper noindex redirect stub and out of the sitemap · `robots.txt` fine · sitemap (41 URLs) exactly matches the real page inventory · `og-preview.html` and `flipandlearn_promo.html` are noindex,nofollow · locale homepages are genuinely fully localized (complete T translations, not thin near-duplicates) · viewport correct everywhere · homepage/blog images have width+height (no CLS risk) · no render-blocking external CSS/JS · consent-gated Ads tag implementation is clean.
