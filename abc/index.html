Nine games, one alphabet

A new way to meet the letters every day.

  • A A

    Letter Match

    Flip the cards and find two of the same letter. Its name is spoken on every match.

  • B b

    Case Match

    Pair every capital letter with its small partner.

  • D

    Word Match

    Match a letter to a picture word that starts with it — animals, fruits and more.

  • Letter Trace

    Trace each letter with a finger, stroke by stroke. A moving dot shows the way first.

  • Balloon Pop

    Pop the balloons that carry the letters of the picture word. A new word every game.

  • Hungry Mo

    Mo is hungry. Feed him the pictures that start with his letter — and wait for the burp.

  • Connect the Dots

    Draw from letter to letter in alphabet order. At the last dot the picture fills with color and comes alive.

  • Letter Fishing

    Listen for the letter, then tap the fish that carries it. Five catches make a game.

  • Sorting Baskets

    Drag each picture into the basket of its first letter. Nothing is timed.

For parents

Made by a parent. Safe by design.

I build Flip & Learn in Regensburg, Germany, for my own son first. ABC has nothing to buy, nothing to sign up for, and it collects no data.

Read the ABC privacy policy

  • No ads

    Nothing pulls your child out of the game.

  • No in-app purchases

    All nine games are included.

  • No data collected

    The app saves only a few settings, and only on the device.

  • Works offline

    No internet connection is needed to play.

  • Parents' area

    Settings and links sit behind a parental gate.

  • Gentle hints

    A lightbulb helps in the three matching games. Switch it off in the parents' area.
