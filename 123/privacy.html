Privacy Policy

The short version: Flip & Learn 123 collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. The app does not use the internet, and the few settings it saves stay on your device.

Who we are

Flip & Learn 123 is a free app for children aged 4 to 7, made by BuraApps. On the home screen it is called “123”. BuraApps is responsible for the app and is the controller for the little personal data described in “If you email us” below. You can reach us at support@flipandlearn.app.

What the app does not collect

  • No personal data: no names, no profiles, no photos, no contacts, no location.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting.
  • No in-app purchases.

What is saved on your device

The app saves a few small values on your device:

  • The three settings in the parents’ area: “Spoken numbers”, “Sound effects” and “Hints”.
  • The game language your child last chose, so the app starts in that language next time.
  • For the parent gate: the number of wrong answers and the time until which the gate stays locked.
  • For the reminder that asks whether you would like to rate the app: a count of how often the app has been started, and the choices made in that reminder.

That is all. The app never sends these values anywhere, and we have no access to them. If you have switched on device backups, Apple or Google may include app settings in your own backup. Deleting the app deletes these values.

Sound and speech

All speech and all sounds in the app are recordings that are bundled inside the app and played from there. If a recording is missing, nothing is spoken: the app does not use your device’s text-to-speech.

While a game is on screen, the app checks once a second whether your device’s sound is turned all the way down (on Android the media volume, on iPhone and iPad the output volume). It uses this only to show a small picture that asks to turn the sound up, and to show the number in writing where a round would otherwise need sound. It does not store or send the volume, and it never changes it.

Internet

Flip & Learn 123 does not use the internet. On Android the app does not request the internet permission, and the app’s own code makes no network calls. The sounds and the fonts are bundled inside the app.

The parents’ area

The parents’ area opens only after an adult has passed the parent gate. Data can leave your device only from there, only when a parent chooses to, and always in another app on your device, never inside Flip & Learn 123:

  • Email to support opens your own email app with a new message addressed to support@flipandlearn.app. The subject line contains the app’s name and version, for example “Flip & Learn 123 — Version 1.0.0 (1)”.
  • Translation feedback (shown only in some menu languages) also opens your email app, with a message to the same address. It contains the code of the menu language, the app’s version and build number, and the question “Which text, and what should it say?”.
  • Flip & Learn: Kids Vocabulary opens the store page of our other app in your store app or browser.
  • Rating asks the operating system to show its rating dialog. The operating system handles that dialog, not the app.
  • Privacy policy opens this page in your browser.

Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email yourself.

If you email us

If you write to support@flipandlearn.app, we receive your email address, your message and anything else your email shows, such as your name.

  • Purpose: we use this only to answer you and to deal with your request.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Sharing: we do not sell or share your email with anyone. It is handled by the email service that delivers our mail, and by no one else.
  • How long we keep it: only as long as we need it to deal with your request. After that we delete it, unless a law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Your rights: you can ask us at any time what we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it or restrict its use, and you can object to its use. Write to support@flipandlearn.app. If you live in the EU you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children’s privacy

Flip & Learn 123 is made for children aged 4 to 7, so it is built to collect nothing from them. In line with the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The app does not collect personal information from children, or from anyone else.
  • The app shows no advertising.
  • Links to stores and to email are only in the parents’ area, behind the parent gate.

App Store and Google Play

You download the app from Apple’s App Store or from Google Play. Apple and Google handle the download and any data connected with it under their own privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. They may show us totals, such as the number of downloads. We cannot identify any person from those figures.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date at the top.
